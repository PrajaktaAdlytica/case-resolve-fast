# Bankxio Site Assets

Startup: Bankxio

Category: fintech operations platform for KYC exceptions, transaction alerts, disputes, complaints, and compliance queues.

## Logo Concept

The Bankxio mark is a calm financial operations control tile. The divided surfaces suggest structured queues and operational workflows, while the subtle X negative space signals exception handling and cross-functional resolution without relying on noisy alert symbols or generic checkmarks.
